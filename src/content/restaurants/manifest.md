## Fine Dining in Liverpool's Baltic Triangle

Manifiest hits all the right notes, a regularly changing tasting menu and a la carte choices, rounded off with a great cheese board and some of the best deserts in town. All with exceptionally well paired wines across the board. The whole team 

Regular "Meniguest" events with guest chefs. You'll need to follow them on Instagram and be ready to jump on bookings as soon as they go live for these, they sell out quicker than Glastonbury tickets.

## Cocktails and Afters
