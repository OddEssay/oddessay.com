## Spicy, Saucy, Crunchy, Vegi: Indian street food and beer"

Everything I love about food at Bundobust: Small plates so you can get a bit of everything you fancy, good beer, and really tasty.

Large tables make it suitable for bigger groups, and means you can often squeeze a couple in without booking.

They brew their own beer at their Manchester Oxford Street, and have a great range of craft beers on offer behind the bar.

## All Vegetarian, stong Vegan options

Everything on the menu is vegetarian, much of the menu - aside from the obvious things like Panneer - is, or can be, Vegan.
