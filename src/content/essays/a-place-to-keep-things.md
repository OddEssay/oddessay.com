## A small beginning

A personal website can begin with a simple idea: give interesting things somewhere to live. A project, a place to eat, a thought that needs more than a few lines.

There is no need for everything to be finished before the first page exists. A small collection can grow one entry at a time.

## Room to return

The useful part of a notebook is being able to find something again. Clear titles, a few thoughtful labels, and ordinary links go a long way.

> Keep a little space for the things you want to come back to.

For now, this page is a starting point. Future essays can take its place, with their own questions and their own conclusions.
